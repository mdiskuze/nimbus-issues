Nimbus - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-24

Nimbus is a hyper-local weather app for a small area on the Swedish coast. It has no account system, no backend server of its own, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. This page explains the only two things that leave your device: where you are, and which weather service that location is sent to.

Location

If you grant Nimbus the location permission, or search for and save a place, the app sends that place's coordinates (latitude and longitude) to one or more of the weather services listed below, so they can answer with a forecast for that spot. Nimbus does not run a server, so nobody but you and that weather service ever sees the request - there is no third party in between collecting or reselling it, and no location history is kept anywhere except on your own device, for your own saved places.

Who Nimbus talks to

Every request below carries only the coordinates needed to answer it (and, for MET Norway, an identifying app version and contact address, which is that service's own requirement for anyone using it). None of them receive a name, an account identifier, or any other data about you, because Nimbus has none to send.

What stays on your device

Saved places, cached forecasts and radar frames, and your app settings are stored locally in the app's own database, so Nimbus can show something even when it cannot reach a network. None of it is uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Notifications

Rain, wind and weather-warning alerts, and the morning brief, are calculated on your device from the same forecast data and shown as a local notification. Nothing about a notification being shown, opened or dismissed is sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Location (coarse/fine) - to fetch the forecast for where you are, if you choose to use that instead of a saved place. Optional; the app works entirely from saved places without it.
  • Notifications - for rain, wind, warning and brief alerts. Can be turned off in the app's own settings or in Android's notification settings.
  • Exact alarm / ignore battery optimisations - so the morning brief and background refresh fire on time on phones that otherwise defer them.
  • Install unknown apps - only in the version distributed outside Google Play (see below); not present in the Play Store build.

Updates, depending on where you got the app

The version installed from Google Play updates only through Google Play, the same as any other Play app. A version installed directly from GitHub instead checks that same public page for a newer release; that check reads a public GitHub URL and downloads nothing but the APK file itself.

Children

Nimbus is a general-audience weather app, not directed at children, and collects no data that would identify or track anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does changes, this page will change with it before that version is released. The date at the top is the last time it was edited.
